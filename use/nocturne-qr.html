xDrip+ Nocturne QR configurator

Fill in the Nocturne cloud upload settings, then scan the QR code from inside xDrip+ (Settings > Import settings from QR code, or QR code from file). xDrip+ shows the list of keys and asks for confirmation before applying.

QR code for xDrip+

Enter the instance URL to generate the QR code.

After scanning

  1. Confirm the import in xDrip+.
  2. Open Settings > Cloud Upload > Nocturne.
  3. Tap Connect to Nocturne: xDrip+ shows an authorization code. Enter it on your Nocturne instance (Connectors & Apps > xDrip+ > Enter Authorization Code).

Authorization tokens are never part of the QR code. They are created on the phone during the connect step.
