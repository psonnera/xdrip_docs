  Smart Watch Features

xDrip direct integration ​

xDrip offers a wide range of smartwatch support.

Android Wear Integration ​

Android Wear Integration

You'll find the most complete integration in Wear OS watches as they are running a part of the xDrip app locally.

The wear extension of xDrip is not under development anymore.

If you need more watchfaces or customizations you can use Tasker (but not as a standalone collector).

Pebble Integration ​

Pebble Integration

Pebble smartwatch use is a historic milestone of CGM in the Cloud. The original Pebble watches were discontinued in 2016 when Pebble was bought by Fitbit (their resources have moved to archives and rebble.io). New Pebble models were relaunched in 2025: xDrip supports both, see Pebble Integration.

AmazFit Sync Service ​

AmazFit Sync Service

AmazFit was used only for Pace and Stratos models.
You cannot use this feature for other AmazFit watches.

BlueJay Watch ​

BlueJay Watch

BlueJay is an incredible companion of your Dexcom sensor, with or without xDrip, with or without phone.

LeFun Band ​

LeFun Band

LeFun band integration was an early implementation for smartbands, best if used in mmol/l.
Unless you still own a LeFun W3, F3, F3S, F11 or F12 band this is not of any use now.

MiBand ​

MiBand

MiBand integration is the result of the huge work of Artem.
Many smartwatches and smartbands are now supported using the companion app WatchDrip+.

Disable MiBand in xDrip if you use WatchDrip+

Use MiBand Band

Smartwatch Sensors ​

xDrip can use supported smartwatch sensors.

Use Health data

xDrip can request the heartbeat sensor to activate and you can smooth that graph.

Heart Rate Sensor

When available you can let xDrip collect health data like heartrate and steps.

Two additional icons will appear on the main view, and if enabled, graphic representations:

  1. A dark blue graph for heartrate
  2. A light cyan cloud line for steps

Other smartwatches ​

More smartwatches and devices can receive data from xDrip even if they are not directly integrated in the app.

Info

In order to have xDrip send data you need to enable xDrip web service.

Android Wear ​

GlucoDataHandler accepts xDrip as a data source. The app is available in Google Play store.

Android Auto ​

GlucoDataAuto is the Android Auto extension of GlucoDataHandler.

Fitbit ​

Google is discontinuing Sense/Ionic/Versa

Consider that these products will be end of life in the near future.

Not available for EU users

Google has removed the possibility since June 2024. Installed watchfaces are not impacted but new users can't install custom watchfaces anymore. You need to use a VPN as a workaround.

Android 9 and below

Android users with a version below 10 need to downgrade the Fitbit app to a previous version like 3.58 and disable automatic updates. You can find it in Aptoide and other apk hosting servers.
